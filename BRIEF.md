# Fallow Ground — Website Brief

## Brand
- **Name:** Fallow Ground
- **Tagline:** "Break up your fallow ground" — Hosea 10:12
- **Author:** Emmett Yeagley
- **Core verse:** Psalm 63:5-8 NKJV

## Mission
A biblical studies and theological platform focused on true discipleship, deep communion with Christ, and the quiet hour that empowers evangelism. Counter-cultural to easy believism. Exegesis, systematic theology, and podcast.

## Design Direction
- Clean, professional, classic refined, high education style
- Think academic journal meets modern web
- Serif typography for headings (like Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond)
- Sans-serif for body (Inter or similar)
- Muted earth tones — deep greens, warm creams, aged paper whites
- Lots of whitespace
- Mobile responsive
- Dark mode support

## Pages
1. **Home** — hero with name + tagline + Psalm 63:5-8, featured articles, about preview
2. **About** — Emmett's bio, mission statement, the meaning behind "Fallow Ground"
3. **Articles** — blog/essay listing page (exegesis, systematic theology)
4. **Podcast** — podcast page with episode listings (placeholder for now)
5. **Newsletter** — placeholder signup (wire up later)

## Tech
- Next.js 15 (App Router)
- Tailwind CSS v4
- TypeScript
- Static/SSG where possible
- Ready for Vercel deployment
- MDX for articles (local content for now, CMS later)
- No database needed yet

## Vibe References
- Desiring God (content depth)
- The Gospel Coalition (layout cleanliness)
- Academic theology journals (typography)
- But more modern and personal than any of those
